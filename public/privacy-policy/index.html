Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal information.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Who we are

Permavia Landscapes ("we", "us") is a regenerative development company based in Hoima City, Uganda. We are responsible for the personal data collected through this website. We process personal data in line with the Uganda Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 and its Regulations.

What we collect

  • Enquiry form: your name, phone number, email, location, land size, project type, service, budget range and message.
  • Waitlist and newsletter forms: your name, email, optional phone number and the course, product or event you are interested in.
  • WhatsApp, phone and email: the information you choose to share with us when you contact us directly.
  • Website usage: anonymous information such as pages visited, device type and approximate location, collected through Google Site Kit (Google Analytics).

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and provide consultations, quotes and services you ask for.
  • To tell you about courses, products and events you signed up to hear about.
  • To improve our website and understand which content is useful.

We never sell your personal data.

Services we use

  • HubSpot: to manage enquiries and client relationships.
  • Mailchimp: to send newsletters and waitlist updates.
  • Google Site Kit / Google Analytics: to measure website traffic.
  • Google Maps: to show our location on the Contact page.

These providers may store data outside Uganda. We only use providers that protect personal data to an adequate standard.

Cookies

Our website uses a small number of cookies for analytics, embedded maps and HubSpot. HubSpot's cookies (such as hubspotutk) recognise returning visitors so that a form you send is linked to your existing record with us. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time; the site will still work.

How long we keep data

We keep enquiry data for as long as needed to serve you and meet legal obligations, and newsletter data until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

You have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, to ask us to correct or delete it, to object to its use for marketing, and to withdraw consent at any time. Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link.

How to request access or deletion

Email info@permavialandscapes.com or call +256 700 000 000. We will respond within the time required by law. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Personal Data Protection Office (PDPO) of Uganda.
