What We Do

What Can We Do For You?

Five ways we transform land, each designed around your site, your goals and your budget.

01

Design Landscapes that Hold Water

Water is the foundation of every living landscape. We design systems that catch the rain where it falls, slow it down and store it in ponds, tanks and, above all, in the soil itself.

  • Swales on contour that sink rain into the ground
  • Ponds and earth dams for animals, irrigation and fish
  • Infiltration basins that recharge groundwater
  • Roof catchment that turns every storm into stored water
02

Restore Living Soils from the Ground Up

Healthy soil is alive. We rebuild it with compost, mulch, cover crops and deep-rooted plants, so fertility grows each season instead of washing away.

  • Compost and biofertilisers made on site
  • Mulching and cover cropping to protect bare ground
  • Soil biology set-up to feed roots naturally
  • Simple monitoring so you can see the change
03

Create Healthy Landscapes

A healthy landscape works with wind and sun, shelters people and wildlife, and stays green through the dry season. We design spaces that feel good to be in and keep improving with time.

  • Windbreaks and shade trees placed for comfort
  • Native species that support birds and pollinators
  • Beautiful, low-maintenance planting
  • Designs that deliver only what your site truly needs
04

Food Forests that Feed You for Generations

Food forests self-regulate once established. Layers of trees, shrubs, herbs and vines grow together as a resilient ecosystem that produces nutrient-dense food for decades with very little work.

  • Fruit, nut, timber and medicinal trees together
  • Seven layers from canopy to roots
  • Year-round harvests and natural pest balance
  • Designed for your climate and your table
05

Guide Your Transition to Regenerative Farming

Moving away from chemical farming is a journey. We walk it with you step by step, protecting your income while the soil, water and biodiversity on your farm recover.

  • A phased plan that protects your cash flow
  • Agroforestry and silvopasture layouts
  • Training for you and your farm team
  • Our aim: reduced input and running costs over time
