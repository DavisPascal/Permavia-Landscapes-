About the Project

Growing the Trees Africa Needs

In short

Million Trees is the tree nursery Permavia Landscapes is establishing in Hoima City, Uganda. It is set up to grow organic, locally adapted seedlings in eight categories (fruit, indigenous, timber, medicinal, herbs and spices, grasses, ornamentals and nitrogen fixers) for farms, homes, schools, NGOs and restoration projects, with the goal of planting millions of trees across East Africa.

Million Trees is the tree nursery we are establishing in Hoima City, Western Uganda. It is set up to propagate fruit, indigenous, timber, medicinal and nitrogen-fixing trees from locally adapted seed and cuttings, so every seedling is ready for the conditions it will grow in.

As the nursery grows, its seedlings will supply Permavia design and farm projects, farmers moving to agroforestry, homes, schools, NGOs and restoration programmes. Every tree is grown organically, without synthetic fertilisers or pesticides. Ask us which species are ready now.

The ambition is simple and big: millions of trees in the ground across East Africa, each one chosen for the right place and the right purpose. We will publish progress here as it happens.

What We Grow

Eight plant categories, each chosen for food, income, soil health or restoration.

Fruit Trees

Mango, avocado, jackfruit, citrus and more for food and income.

Indigenous Trees

Native species that restore habitat and suit local conditions.

Timber & Woodlot

Fast-growing and hardwood species for timber, poles and fuel.

Medicinal Plants

Trees and shrubs valued in traditional and modern medicine.

Herbs & Spices

Culinary and aromatic plants for kitchens and markets.

Grasses & Groundcover

Fodder grasses and living mulch that protect bare soil.

Ornamentals

Beautiful flowering plants for homes, hotels and campuses.

Nitrogen Fixers

Leguminous trees that feed the soil and the plants around them.

Questions

Questions About Seedlings

Where can I buy tree seedlings in Hoima?

From the Million Trees nursery that Permavia Landscapes is establishing in Hoima City. The nursery is still being set up, so request a price list or message us on WhatsApp to hear which species are ready.

What kinds of tree seedlings does Million Trees grow?

Eight categories: fruit trees, indigenous trees, timber and woodlot trees, medicinal plants, herbs and spices, grasses and groundcover, ornamentals, and nitrogen-fixing trees.

Are the seedlings organic?

Yes. Every seedling is grown without synthetic fertilisers or pesticides, from seed and cuttings collected from healthy local trees, so it is adapted to local conditions from day one.

Who can order seedlings?

Farmers moving to agroforestry, homeowners, schools, NGOs, restoration and carbon projects, and Permavia’s own design clients.

Can Permavia help plan where to plant the trees?

Yes. Our Farm Solutions and Regenerative Design services choose the right species for the right place on your land.
