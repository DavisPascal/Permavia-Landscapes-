About This Service

What It Involves

In short

Installation of Regenerative & Community Development Projects from Permavia Landscapes. Turning plans into functioning landscapes and projects, from site preparation and planting to community-led environmental and livelihood initiatives. Available in Hoima, Masindi, Kikuube, Kagadi, Buliisa, Kampala and across East Africa, using organic, nature-based methods only.

Our trained field teams bring designs to life, on private land and in community development projects. We shape swales and ponds, prepare living soils, plant native and productive species, and install water-wise irrigation. For community projects we work alongside local people, so skills and ownership stay in the community. We manage the work from first dig to final planting, with clear milestones so you always know where your project stands.

Typical Activities

  • Site preparation, marking out and earthworks (swales, ponds, terraces)
  • Soil preparation: compost, mulch and soil biology
  • Planting trees, food forests, gardens and ground cover
  • Water systems: irrigation, roof catchment and storage
  • Community mobilisation, training and shared planting days
  • Clear milestones, progress updates and a handover pack

How a Project Runs

The Implementation Journey

Six stages from first visit to a landscape or project that you, or the community, can run.

  1. 01

    Assessment

    We visit the site, confirm the design and agree goals, scope and budget with you and your partners.

  2. 02

    Planning

    A work plan with phases, materials, people, timing and the season to plant in.

  3. 03

    Mobilisation

    We bring together the team, tools, seedlings and materials, and involve community members from the start.

  4. 04

    Installation

    Earthworks, soil preparation, water systems and planting, built to the design.

  5. 05

    Establishment

    Watering, mulching and replacement planting while the system takes root.

  6. 06

    Handover

    A walk-through, a simple care guide and training so you or the community can manage it.

Partners & Communities

Communities as Partners

On community and development projects we work with NGOs, local government, schools and community groups as partners, not just beneficiaries. Local people help shape the plan, take part in the work and are trained to look after it, so the project keeps going after we step back.
