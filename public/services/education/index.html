About This Service

What It Involves

In short

Education for Regenerative Design & Management from Permavia Landscapes. Building practical knowledge and skills through training, demonstrations, workshops, learning resources and online education. Available across East Africa, putting nature-based methods first.

We train individuals, staff teams and communities to design and manage regenerative land themselves, from site design and water harvesting to running food forests, carbon markets and programme design. Training is hands-on and rooted in East African conditions. It is delivered on your site, at partner venues or online, and on Permavia Farm once it opens.

Learning Outcomes

What You Will Be Able to Do

  • Read a site and draw a simple regenerative design
  • Plan and build water-harvesting earthworks
  • Make compost and rebuild living soil
  • Establish and manage food forests and agroforestry
  • Plan, run and monitor a regenerative project

Questions About Education

What does Education for Regenerative Design & Management include?

Building practical knowledge and skills through training, demonstrations, workshops, learning resources and online education. Typical work includes: landscape design training, carbon markets, programme design, leadership & facilitation.

Who is it for?

Individuals, NGO Staff, Corporate Teams, Government. We work across East Africa.
