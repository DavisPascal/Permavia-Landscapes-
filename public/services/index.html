What We Offer

Five Ways We Help You Regenerate

From the first site walk to a thriving, self-sustaining landscape, choose the service that fits where you are today.

01Design02Installation03Farm & Agroforestry04Maintenance05Education
In short

Permavia Landscapes offers five regenerative services to clients across Uganda and East Africa: landscape design, installation, farm and agroforestry solutions, landscape maintenance and education and training. Every project uses organic, nature-based methods, and every client can start with a free 30-minute discovery call.

01

Regenerative Landscape Design

Every great landscape starts with careful observation. We walk your land, read its slopes, soils, water flows, wind and sun, then produce a clear, buildable design that turns problems into resources. You receive maps, planting plans and a phased budget you can act on, whether you build with us or on your own.

Site AssessmentWater & Keyline DesignFood Forest PlanningClimate-Adaptive Layouts
Ideal for: Homeowners, Farmers, Businesses, NGOs
Request a consultation
02

Installation & Regenerative Projects

Our trained field teams bring designs to life. We shape swales and ponds, prepare living soils, plant native and productive species, and install water-wise irrigation. We manage the work from first dig to final planting, with clear milestones so you always know where your project stands.

Earthworks & SwalesNative PlantingIrrigation SystemsSoil Biology Setup
Ideal for: Eco-developers, Resorts, Farms, Corporate Campuses

03

Farm & Agroforestry Solutions

We help farms move away from costly inputs towards systems that build fertility year after year. Trees, crops and animals are integrated so each supports the other: shade, mulch, fodder, fruit, timber and income from the same land. Plans are practical, phased and matched to your markets.

Agroforestry PlanningSilvopastureSoil RestorationCarbon Sequestration

Questions

Questions About Our Services

What does a regenerative landscape designer do?

A regenerative landscape designer plans land so that it improves over time: catching and storing rainwater, rebuilding living soil, and combining trees, food plants and animals so they support each other. At Permavia we assess your site, produce a buildable design, and can install, maintain and train your team on it.

Which service should I start with?

Most clients start with Regenerative Landscape Design, because a good plan saves money on everything that follows. If you are unsure, book a free 30-minute discovery call on Webex and we will recommend the right first step.

Do you only design, or do you also build and look after the landscape?

We do all four: design, build (earthworks, planting, irrigation), maintain (seasonal care and annual health checks) and educate (hands-on training). You can use one service or all of them as one accountable team.

Which areas of Uganda do you work in?

We are based in Hoima City and work regularly in Hoima, Masindi, Kikuube, Kagadi, Buliisa, Kampala. For larger projects we travel across Uganda and East Africa, and we can design remotely using maps, photos and video calls.

Can you help a farm move to agroforestry or regenerative farming?

Yes. Our Farm & Agroforestry Solutions service plans a phased move away from costly inputs, protecting your income while trees, crops and animals are integrated to rebuild soil fertility.

Do you use chemicals or synthetic fertilisers?

No. All our services use organic, nature-based methods only: compost, mulch, cover crops, nitrogen-fixing trees and natural pest balance.
