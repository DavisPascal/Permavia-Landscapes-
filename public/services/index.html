In short

Permavia Landscapes offers five regenerative services to clients across Uganda and East Africa: regenerative design, installation of regenerative and community development projects, farm solutions such as agroforestry, syntropic agroforestry and food forests, maintenance and education for regenerative design and management. Every project uses organic, nature-based methods, and every client can start with a free 30-minute discovery call.

Questions

Questions About Our Services

What does a regenerative landscape designer do?

A regenerative landscape designer plans land so that it improves over time: catching and storing rainwater, rebuilding living soil, and combining trees, food plants and animals so they support each other. At Permavia we assess your site, produce a buildable design, and can install, maintain and train your team on it.

Which service should I start with?

Most people start with Regenerative Design, because a good plan saves money on everything that follows. If you are unsure, book a free 30-minute discovery call on Webex and we will recommend the right first step.

Do you only design, or do you also build and look after the landscape?

We do all of it: design, installation (earthworks, planting, water systems), farm solutions, maintenance (seasonal care and yearly reviews) and education (hands-on training). You can use one service or all of them as one accountable team.

Which areas of Uganda do you work in?

We are based in Hoima City and work regularly in Hoima, Masindi, Kikuube, Kagadi, Buliisa, Kampala. For larger projects we travel across Uganda and East Africa, and we can design remotely using maps, photos and video calls.

Can you help a farm move to agroforestry or regenerative farming?

Yes. Our Farm Solutions service plans a phased move away from costly inputs, protecting your income while agroforestry, syntropic agroforestry, food forests or silvopasture rebuild soil fertility, all in line with permaculture.

Do you work with NGOs, government and development partners?

Yes. Our Installation of Regenerative & Community Development Projects service is built for this: we plan and deliver environmental and livelihood projects with communities as active partners, and train local people to manage them.

Do you use chemicals or synthetic fertilisers?

No. All our services use organic, nature-based methods only: compost, mulch, cover crops, nitrogen-fixing trees and natural pest balance.
