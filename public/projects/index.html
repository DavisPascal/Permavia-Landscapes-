Our Projects

What Permavia Is Building

Our services are what you can hire us to do. Our projects are the long-term initiatives we are building ourselves: a tree nursery, a demonstration farm and a learning platform.

Explore Our Projects Discuss a Partnership
In short

Permavia Landscapes is building three projects in Uganda: Million Trees, a tree nursery (underway); Permavia Farm, a regenerative demonstration farm (planning); and Permavia Academy, a learning platform for regenerative design and management (in development). None is complete yet, and each page shows its real stage.
