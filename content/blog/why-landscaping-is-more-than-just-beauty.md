For a long time, landscaping meant lawns, hedges and flower beds that looked good and asked a lot in return: water, fertiliser, labour. Regenerative landscaping keeps the beauty but changes the deal. Your garden starts working for you.

## Can a garden be beautiful and feed you?

Fruit trees, perennial vegetables, herbs and climbing beans can be arranged as attractively as any ornamental garden. In a food forest, layers of plants grow together the way they do in a natural forest: tall canopy trees, smaller fruit trees, shrubs, herbs, groundcover and roots. Once established, this system produces food year after year with little digging.

## A landscape that cools and protects

Well-placed trees shade walls and roofs, cutting indoor temperatures. Windbreaks shelter homes and crops from wind and sun. Groundcover protects soil from the hard rains that otherwise wash it away.

- **Lower costs:** less bought-in water, fertiliser and food over time.
- **Healthier soil:** mulch and diverse roots feed soil life instead of depleting it.
- **More life:** birds, bees and beneficial insects return and keep pests in balance.

## Beauty with purpose

None of this means giving up on beauty. The most memorable landscapes are the ones that feel alive: shaded paths, the scent of fruit blossom, water moving through the land. That is the kind of beauty that grows richer with every season.
